# OneShield

> OneShield Software provides core insurance platform solutions for property and casualty (P&C) insurers, managing general agents (MGAs), program administrators, and insurance startups. Its OneShield Enterprise and OneShield Market Solutions (OMS) platforms cover policy administration, billing, claims, rating, product configuration, and analytics across 90+ lines of business, exposing RESTful and SOAP integration services (XML/JSON). API access is provisioned to customers and partners; there is no public developer portal or OpenAPI.

## Products
- [OneShield Enterprise](https://oneshield.com/products-our-solutions/oneshield-enterprise-solution/enterprise-overview/): Comprehensive cloud/SaaS core insurance suite.
- [OneShield Market Solutions (OMS)](https://oneshield.com/products-our-solutions/oneshield-market-solutions/): Preconfigured SaaS with an API integration suite for policy, billing, and claims.
